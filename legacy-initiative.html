Educational Legacy Initiative

Connecting Veterans and Students Through Historical Preservation

Our Vision

The Agent Orange Awareness Memorial Archive was established to preserve the stories and sacrifices of Vietnam Era veterans while creating an educational resource for future generations.

This initiative seeks to connect local veterans with students through oral histories, storytelling, historical research, photography, videography, and digital archiving.

Student Collaboration

A central goal of this project is collaboration with students from the Vidal M. Treviño School of Communication and Fine Arts. Students will have opportunities to participate in oral history interviews, documentary projects, photography, and multimedia storytelling while helping preserve local military history.

Future Expansion

While the archive currently focuses on Agent Orange awareness and Vietnam Era veterans, future phases of the project will include veterans from additional periods of military service and conflict.

The long-term vision is to build a permanent community archive that preserves military service stories from multiple generations.

Community-Wide Partnership

Future development will explore participation from students throughout the community, encouraging collaboration among local schools, veterans, families, historians, and community organizations.

By combining education and historical preservation, this initiative seeks to ensure that the stories of local veterans remain accessible for future generations.
